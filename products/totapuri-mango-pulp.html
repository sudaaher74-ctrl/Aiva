Product FAQs

Why choose Totapuri over Alphonso for beverages?

Totapuri has a more tart flavor and is highly cost-effective, making it the perfect base for blended juices where pure Alphonso might be too expensive.

Overview

Totapuri Mango Pulp is widely preferred in the food processing industry for its ideal blend of sweetness and acidity. Its cost-effectiveness and strong flavor make it the perfect base ingredient for multi-fruit nectars and industrial blends.

Applications

Beverage bases, multi-fruit juices, jams, and fruit yogurts.
