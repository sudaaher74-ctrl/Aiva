Product FAQs

What is the shelf life of Alphonso Mango Pulp?

When packed in aseptic bags inside steel drums, our Alphonso Mango Pulp has a shelf life of 24 months at ambient temperatures.

Do you add any artificial colors or preservatives?

No, our pulp is 100% natural. The aseptic processing technology allows us to preserve it without any artificial additives.

Overview

Our Alphonso Mango Pulp is extracted from premium, hand-picked Alphonso mangoes sourced directly from the famous Ratnagiri and Devgad orchards in India. Known globally as the 'King of Mangoes', this pulp boasts a vibrant golden-yellow color, rich aroma, and an unparalleled sweet flavor profile.

Applications

Perfect for premium fruit juices, nectars, yogurts, ice creams, bakery fillings, and artisanal confectionery.
