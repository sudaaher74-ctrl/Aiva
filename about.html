Who We Are

Global Leaders in Premium Food Export

Founded with a vision to streamline the global supply chain for premium fruit products, AIVA Enterprises has grown into a trusted partner for top food manufacturers worldwide.

We operate at the intersection of traditional agriculture and modern food technology. By partnering directly with farmers and utilizing state-of-the-art aseptic and IQF processing facilities, we ensure that the natural goodness of every fruit is preserved from farm to destination port.

Our Mission

To deliver uncompromised quality in food ingredients globally, fostering sustainable agricultural practices, and ensuring reliable, transparent supply chains for our international B2B partners.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted and preferred sourcing partner for premium Indian agricultural products, recognized for our integrity, innovation in processing, and unparalleled customer service.
