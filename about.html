The Origin Story

Cultivating Global Horizons From Humble Roots

AIVA Enterprises was founded in 2009 by Abhinav Patel, a second-generation agricultural consultant who noticed a critical gap in the international food manufacturing supply chain. While India grew the sweetest Alphonso mangoes, global buyers struggled with inconsistent Brix levels, acidity fluctuations, and sub-standard packaging during transit.

With a small processing unit in Maharashtra and a firm commitment to standardizing natural purees, Abhinav pioneered clean aseptic processing technologies. He worked directly with regional farming cooperatives to implement Good Agricultural Practices (GAP), guaranteeing transparent traceability from farm gates to destination ports.

Standardized for Excellence

"Our first export was a modest consignment of Kesar mango pulp to Europe. Today, we process over 50,000 Metric Tons annually, yet our core premise remains identical: natural purity meeting standard industrial precision."

Over fifteen years, AIVA Enterprises expanded from aseptic pulps to high-brix concentrates and state-of-the-art IQF individual quick freezing tunnels. Under Abhinav's stewardship, we have established trusted footholds across North America, Europe, Australia, and the Middle East, transforming how the global HoReCa and manufacturing sectors source premium tropical ingredients.
