Our Process

From Orchard to
Ocean Freight

A vertically integrated process ensuring every batch meets the strictest international quality and food safety standards.

01

Farm Sourcing

Direct procurement from GAP-certified farms across India's premier growing regions. Seasonal planning ensures peak-ripeness sourcing for maximum Brix and flavor.

02

Quality Inspection

Multi-point quality checks — Brix, acidity, pH, color grading, and microbial testing — before any raw material enters the processing line.

03

Processing & Packaging

State-of-the-art aseptic processing, evaporation, and IQF freezing lines. Automated filling into drums, aseptic bags, and retail packs under sterile conditions.

04

Cold Chain & Export

Temperature-controlled warehousing and reefer container logistics to major ports. Full documentation — phytosanitary certificates, COA, Bill of Lading — handled in-house.

Global Reach

Supplying Worldwide
